## YP Soundwave — the current logo

<figure class="logo-figure logo-dark"><img src="/assets/img/logos/yp-soundwave.png" alt="YP Soundwave logo: the letters Y and P stitched together by a sound wave" width="512" height="335"></figure>

My current logo is built, like the first one, from my initials — **Y** and **P**. This time the letters are stitched together by a sound wave: a single line runs through both of them, rises and falls like a waveform, and joins the two letters into one sign.

It is the simplest way I know to say what this site is about: a name and a sound, written in the same stroke.

## YP Monogram — the colour logo

<figure class="logo-figure logo-light"><img src="/assets/img/logos/yp-monogram.png" alt="YP colour monogram: the letters Y and P written as one symmetrical hourglass" width="458" height="476"></figure>

My earlier logo has a story of its own. It is formed by the Latin letters **Y** and **P**, written as one symmetrical cursive stroke: the right half of the first letter is at the same time the left half of the second.

Written this way, the two letters form an **hourglass** — a symbol of eternity.

The colours of its segments stand for the different **elements**, and the small **white triangle** that appears between the strokes stands for the **mountains** — the earth as an element of its own.
