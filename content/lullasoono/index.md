## Music and stories from the Moon

**LullaSoono** is a musical world of two gentle lunar beings — **Lulla** and **Soono**.

They live quietly on the Moon, watching Earth with the same warmth and curiosity with which we look up at the night sky.

Through their voices and melodies, they share moments of calm, play, and dreams — small stories carried by sound and light.

Their music drifts between lullabies, songs, and sound tales.

Some pieces help to fall asleep, others invite to move, imagine, or simply listen.

Each track is like a fragment of moonlight, finding its way to those who still believe in wonder.

<div class="cover" style="background-image:url('banner-2-small.jpg')" markdown="1">

**Lulla** is bright and curious — a spark of moonlight that never stands still.  
She hums while she flies, wakes the stars when they fall asleep too early, and turns every sound she finds into a little melody. Her laughter echoes across the craters like silver wind.  
  
**Soono** is quiet and round, a gentle dreamer who listens more than he speaks. He keeps a bell by his side and collects soft sounds — the hum of starlight, the hush of dust, the breath of the Moon. When Lulla sings, he adds the harmony that feels like night itself.  
  
Together they fill the Moon with warmth and quiet joy. Their music drifts between play and stillness, between dreams and the gentle glow of things unspoken.

</div>

## What you’ll hear

- **Lullabies** for rest and bedtime
- **Playful tunes** for daytime adventures
- **Dreamscapes** that mix voice, synths, and gentle percussion
- **Stories and sound tales** told through music and whisper

Each release brings another glimpse of their lunar world — its nights, dreams, and glowing silence.

## Mood & Style

Soft · Dreamy · Warm · Curious · Safe

A blend of ambient textures, acoustic elements, and simple electronic tones shaped to feel both intimate and spacious.

LullaSoono’s music is not about perfection — it’s about kindness, imagination, and the comfort of slowing down.
