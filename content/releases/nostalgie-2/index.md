**Nostalgie** is a mini-album that blends French motifs, deep emotions, and cinematic storytelling. Across its three tracks, it explores themes of **love, fate, memory, and longing**, inviting listeners into a world where the past lingers, love transforms, and time moves in mysterious loops.

At its core, **Nostalgie** is both a **tribute and a reflection**—a space where music intertwines with personal history, nostalgia becomes both healing and haunting, and melodies carry stories that words alone cannot tell.

## The Symbolism of the Album Cover

The cover art features a **soaring eagle**, painted in **red, white, and blue**—a nod to the **French flag**, reflecting the album’s language and themes. But beyond that, the eagle represents a deeper tribute:

> <pre class="lyrics">Ainsi, quand les ailes se brisent,
> Nulle chute n’efface leur grise.
> Dans le ciel, ils volent toujours,
> Revenant dans nos amours.</pre>

Even when wings break, they continue to fly in our hearts. This album carries that sentiment—**a tribute to someone whose presence remains in memory and music.**
