## Mother of All – A Tribute to Our Planet

**“Mother of All”** is more than just a song—it’s a poetic ode to the greatest force in our existence: Nature herself. The track, featured in my album **“[Virgin Natura](/releases/virgin-natura/)“**, is a heartfelt exploration of the bond between humanity and the Earth, the giver of life and sustainer of all.

This song reflects on the beauty, power, and resilience of our planet, personifying her as a fierce, nurturing mother. From her flowing rivers and fertile soil to her fiery core and tumultuous storms, Nature stands as a testament to life’s complexity and endurance.

Through the verses, **“Mother of All”** speaks of Nature’s generosity and the cost of humanity’s exploitation. It celebrates her beauty while also serving as a stark reminder of her wrath and the need for balance. Her love is unconditional, but her patience is not infinite. How long can she forgive us for taking her gifts for granted?

The lyrics invite listeners to reflect on our relationship with Nature. They remind us to cherish her warmth, taste her rain, and marvel at her boundless power. At the same time, they pose a haunting question: can we mend our ways before it’s too late?

The full lyrics of the song are shared below. Allow them to guide you into a deeper connection with the Earth, the ultimate **Mother of All**.
