> *“Sometimes, from their home on the Moon, Lulla and Soono look down at the quiet Earth and sing for those who are falling asleep.* *And sometimes they drift down to the world below — to touch the grass, to listen, to dream with us.* *On nights like these, they look back at their own Moon and smile —* *for even from Earth, it shines the same.”*

## About the Album

*Dreamy Nights Vol. 1* is the first musical story from **[LullaSoono](/lullasoono/)** — two lunar beings who watch the Earth and share what they feel through sound.

Each track is a small nighttime scene: from the last glow of sunset to the hush before morning.

The music lives somewhere between ambient, lullaby, and imagination — a calm, slow-moving world made of air, light, and friendship.

Some songs are sung by **Soono**, others by **Lulla**, and a few let their voices drift together like twin reflections on moonlight.

It’s an album to fall asleep to — or to simply breathe and dream with.
