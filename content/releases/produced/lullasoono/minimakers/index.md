Welcome to MiniMakers, a warm and playful album by [LullaSoono](/lullasoono/) — featuring Lulla (the moon girl) and Soono (the moon boy), two lovable Moon Friends who bring gentle music and cozy stories to kids and families.

MiniMakers was created as a soundtrack for creative play: drawing, building, crafting, and all those magical moments when imagination takes the lead. Whether your little one is coloring a new world, stacking blocks into a rocket ship, or making a “masterpiece” out of paper and glue, this album is designed to feel like a friendly musical companion — calm, cheerful, and inspiring.

## What Mini Makers feels like

MiniMakers is kid-friendly background music that supports focus and fun at the same time. It’s not “too busy,” so it doesn’t distract — but it’s still full of bright, sweet details that keep the mood happy and curious.

Great for:

- Drawing and coloring
- Crafts, DIY, cutting and gluing
- Blocks, building, and creative construction
- Puzzles and board games
- Calm playtime at home

### Meet the Moon Friends: Lulla and Soono

Lulla and Soono are gentle, imaginative characters from the Moon — a little duo that kids can recognize and feel safe with. On the official LullaSoono channel and releases, they bring a cozy world where music helps children feel relaxed, creative, and ready to play.
