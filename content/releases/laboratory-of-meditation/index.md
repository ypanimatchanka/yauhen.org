I am thrilled to announce the release of my latest music project, an ambient album titled “Laboratory of Meditation”. This album marks the beginning of a new series called “Laboratory of”, through which I aim to explore and publish music that not only possesses an intriguing sound but also fits into specific listening categories. For “Laboratory of Meditation”, the focus has been on crafting meditative melodies that invite listeners into a space of introspection and tranquility.

The concept behind “Laboratory of Meditation” is quite unique – each track is named “Experiment”, followed by its respective part number. This nomenclature is a nod to the experimental nature of the music, as each piece serves as an exploration into the vast expanse of meditative genres. The album consists of eight tracks, each with its own distinct ambiance and duration:

1. Experiment, Pt. 1 – 2:45
2. Experiment, Pt. 2 – 2:39
3. Experiment, Pt. 3 – 2:38
4. Experiment, Pt. 4 – 2:45
5. Experiment, Pt. 5 – 2:32
6. Experiment, Pt. 6 – 2:40
7. Experiment, Pt. 7 – 3:16
8. Experiment, Pt. 8 – 2:31

Through “Laboratory of Meditation”, I sought to blend serene soundscapes with the essence of ambient music to create a collection that encourages deep reflection and relaxation. Each piece, or “Experiment”, has been carefully crafted to offer a unique auditory experience, guiding listeners through a journey of calm and introspection.

The album is now available across various streaming platforms. I invite you to immerse yourself in the tranquil world of “Laboratory of Meditation”. Whether you are seeking a soundtrack for your meditation practice, looking for calming background music, or simply wish to explore the soothing sounds of ambient music, this album is for you.

As the first installment in the “Laboratory of” series, “Laboratory of Meditation” sets the tone for future explorations into different soundscapes and listening experiences. I am excited to continue this journey, experimenting with sounds and melodies that resonate on a deeper level.

Thank you for your support and for joining me on this musical adventure. I hope “Laboratory of Meditation” brings you peace, reflection, and a moment of calm in the hustle and bustle of everyday life.
