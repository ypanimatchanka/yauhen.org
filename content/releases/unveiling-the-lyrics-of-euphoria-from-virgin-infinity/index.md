As we embark on the journey of “[Virgin Infinity](/releases/virgin-infinity-the-first-album-of-a-new-musical-cycle/)“, we are excited to present the lyrics of one of the standout tracks from the album, “Euphoria.” This song encapsulates the essence of pure, unadulterated joy and the transcendental experience of losing oneself in the moment.

“Euphoria” invites you to get lost under the night sky, feel the beat, and let go of your fears. It’s a celebration of the present, where the rhythm and light elevate us, allowing us to soar. This track perfectly captures the spirit of our new thematic cycle, emphasizing the untouched, pristine beauty of existence.
