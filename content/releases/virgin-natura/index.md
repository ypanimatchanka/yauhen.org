### 🎶 Introducing the Second Chapter of the “Virgin” Series: “Virgin Natura” 🌿

I am delighted to present my new musical album, **“Virgin Natura”**, the second installment in the thematic cycle titled **“Virgin”**, which began with the release of **“[Virgin Infinity](/releases/virgin-infinity-the-first-album-of-a-new-musical-cycle/)“**. This series seeks to honor and explore the beauty and essence of femininity and its profound connections with the natural world.

**“Virgin Natura”** delves into the purity and power of nature through a uniquely feminine lens. Across four immersive tracks, this album invites listeners to reflect on the relationship between humanity and the Earth, celebrating nature as a nurturing yet vulnerable mother figure while revealing the stark realities of how humanity interacts with its life-giving source.

#### 🎵 Tracklist:

- **[Intro](/releases/intro-in-virgin-natura/)** – A melodic and ambient opening with gentle vocals, setting the tone for the album’s journey into the feminine essence of nature.
- **[Viva Natura](/releases/viva-natura/)** – A tribute to the vibrancy and magnificence of nature, portraying its spirit and beauty as a woman.
- **[Mother of All](/releases/mother-of-all/)** – A powerful exploration of nature as humanity’s nurturing mother, endlessly giving despite the toll humanity takes.
- **[Child Eater](/releases/child-eater/)** – A provocative finale that portrays humanity as a voracious force, veiling destruction with a facade of care and concern.

Each track invites contemplation and emotional connection, blending melody, meaning, and powerful imagery to create a truly unforgettable listening experience.
