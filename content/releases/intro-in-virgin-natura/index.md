## “Intro” – The Gateway to *Virgin Natura*

Silence. Depth. The first step. This is how the journey into my new album *[Virgin Natura](/releases/virgin-natura/)* begins. “Intro” is not merely an opening track; it’s a gentle portal leading you into vast forests, timeless streams, and the breath of nature itself.

This ambient composition combines serene melodies with the delicate interplay of male and female vocals. It invites you to pause, to feel, and to step into a realm where harmony flows effortlessly, and nature speaks its own language.

## LYRICS:

<pre class="lyrics">In quiet depths, where silence flows,
A hidden world that softly grows.
Untouched paths and timeless streams,
Nature stirs in woven dreams.

Leaves entwine, roots run deep,
In forests vast, where secrets sleep.
She waits, a breath, a gentle hum,
Where earth and spirit are as one.

Step inside this ancient grace,
Feel her warmth, her wild embrace.
Unbound beauty, raw and true,
Nature’s song, she breathes for you.</pre>

“Intro” is a musical breath of fresh air, transporting you to an ancient forest where each note is a rustling leaf and every vocal harmony is the heartbeat of the earth. It sets the tone for *[Virgin Natura](/releases/virgin-natura/)*, inviting you to begin this journey gently and gracefully—like the soft unfolding of dawn.

The story starts here. Let “Intro” lead you into the embrace of *[Virgin Natura](/releases/virgin-natura/)*.
