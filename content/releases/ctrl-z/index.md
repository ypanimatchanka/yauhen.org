Can we ever go back? Can we erase the cracks, reset the map, and bring back the world as we remember it?

*“CTRL + Z”* dives into one of the most **primal instincts** of the human mind—the relentless desire to rewind time. Every generation believes the past was better—skies were bluer, life was simpler, loved ones were closer. We chase memories, desperate to undo mistakes, to relive moments, to escape the march of time. But no matter how many times we try to press *Undo*, the past remains out of reach.

No power, no belief, no command can change that. Whether you trust **CTRL + Z** or **CMD + Z**, whether you hold onto one truth or another, the result is the same—there is no way back. The present cannot be undone.

<div class="embed embed-apple" data-provider="apple" data-src="https://embed.music.apple.com/pl/album/ctrl-z/1809330946?i=1809330948"></div>

This track is a key piece of *[Virgin Mind](/releases/virgin-mind/)*—an album that explores the untouched, **raw nature of human thought and emotion**. The longing to return, to fix, to hold onto what’s slipping away has always been part of us. It’s not just nostalgia—it’s something deeper, something woven into our consciousness. *CTRL + Z* captures that struggle, that hope, and the final, inevitable realization: history doesn’t reload.
