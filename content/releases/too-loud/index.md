## When Everything Fights for Your Attention

The world never shuts up. Advertisements tell you what to buy. Social media tells you how to think. Strangers argue about things you don’t even care about. Somewhere in all this chaos, your own thoughts get drowned out.

**“Too Loud”** is about that feeling—the moment when you realize you don’t even know which opinions are yours anymore. It’s not just about sound; it’s about mental overload. Every voice competes for space in your head, and before you know it, there’s no room left for yourself.

<div class="embed embed-apple" data-provider="apple" data-src="https://embed.music.apple.com/pl/album/too-loud/1809330946?i=1809330949"></div>

This track is part of [Virgin Mind](/releases/virgin-mind/), an album about a mind before the world fills it with noise. In the beginning, everything is clear. But then the pressure starts—fit in, follow the rules, be like everyone else. The song moves between quiet reflection and bursts of frustration, just like the way thoughts build up until they finally explode.

At some point, you just want silence. But in today’s world, is that even possible?
