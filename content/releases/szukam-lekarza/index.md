Discover the heartfelt journey of a migrant navigating life in Poland through the lens of “Szukam Lekarza,” a song from the album “Dziennik Cudzoziemca”. This lyric video portrays the challenges of seeking medical help as a foreigner, reflecting on struggles with access, cost, and communication.

In this poignant narrative, the protagonist — a young woman — reveals a unique perspective: while others dream of finding a prince, she dreams of finding a doctor who can heal and guide her. Through its poetic lyrics and emotive melody, “Szukam Lekarza” shines a light on the everyday battles and aspirations of migrants striving for a better life.

🩺✨ “For some, love is the ultimate dream; for me, it’s the hope of care and understanding.”
