**LUMINŌX** is a new single from the ongoing musical anthology **[ZOOdiac](/anthologies/zoodiac/)**.

This project consists of twelve tracks, each dedicated to a fictional creature that forms an alternative, imaginative zodiac. Every animal in the anthology represents a unique artistic concept and appears only within this musical universe.

**Luminōx** is presented as a glowing fox-like being associated with night, forest shadows, and quiet illumination. The track is built around Latin-inspired vocals, choral textures, and a pronounced rhythmic foundation. The overall idea of the piece is connected to the atmosphere surrounding the character itself — a creature that appears in darkness and carries its own inner light.

## Creative inspiration

While working on **LUMINŌX**, I drew inspiration from several sources:

- **Vocal and stylistic traditions of projects such as Gregorian and other acts working with Latin-based choral aesthetics**  
  *Not in a religious sense, but in the way these projects combine voices, harmony, and an ancient mood.*
- **The expressive quality of Latin and pseudo-Latin lyrical forms**  
  *The language itself contributes a sense of age, structure, and artistic distance, which fits the fictional setting of ZOOdiac.*
- **The visual and mythological idea of a luminous creature**  
  *The concept of a fox that appears in deep darkness and emits its own light became the basis for the artistic mood of the release.*

The track includes choral elements, vocal lines, and a strong percussive layer, forming the characteristic tone of the piece without describing the sound beyond what is directly present.
