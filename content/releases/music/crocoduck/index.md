Released as the second sign in my ongoing anthology **[Zoodiac](/anthologies/zoodiac/)**, *Crocoduck* continues the idea of a **musical horoscope** built around imaginary creatures — hybrid beings that exist somewhere between instinct, humor, and myth.

If *[Hypnopotamus](/releases/hypnopotamus/)* explored slow hypnosis and calm depth, *Crocoduck* swings to the opposite pole — sharp, playful, unpredictable.  
The name itself — a fusion of *crocodile* and *duck* — already sounds absurd, but that absurdity hides something familiar: the duality between charm and danger, between laughter and fear.

Musically, *Crocoduck* is built on **choppy percussive grooves**, **glitched vocal layers**, and **biting synth textures** that echo the song’s personality — funny at first glance, then unsettling once you get too close.  
It’s a sonic paradox: half comedy, half cautionary tale.

Lyrically, it toys with irony — a creature that smiles and jokes, but whose grin hides teeth.  
That tension between innocence and threat runs through the entire track: *“Smile with a snap — he’s never just a quack.”*  
It’s both a cartoon and a mirror.

With *Crocoduck*, I wanted to capture that feeling when something looks lighthearted but carries an edge — the moment laughter turns into awareness.  
It’s a reminder that even the most absurd masks can bite back.

*Listen to Crocoduck and meet the second sign of [Zoodiac](/anthologies/zoodiac/) — where humor flutters, and danger winks.*
