### Music production under the YAUHEN.ORG label

I’m **Yauhen Panimatchanka** — a composer, songwriter, and music producer.

Beyond my own releases, I also produce music for **other fictional and conceptual artists**, each with a unique identity, sound, and story. These projects are released under my independent label **YAUHEN.ORG**.

My approach to producing is deeply personal:

- I don’t just mix and master — I build entire worlds around sound.
- Each artist project begins with a single idea, then grows into melodies, lyrics, visuals, and eventually a living persona.

#### What I do

- **Concept & composition** – transforming an idea into sound and emotion
- **Songwriting & arrangement** – building melodies, harmonies, and lyrics that stay in memory
- **Sound design & production** – shaping the tone and atmosphere for each project
- **Release management** – distribution, artwork, and digital presence across platforms
- **Creative direction** – guiding the artist’s visual and emotional identity

## LullaSoono

**LullaSoono** is a musical world of two gentle lunar beings — **Lulla** and **Soono**.  
They live quietly on the Moon, watching Earth with the same warmth and curiosity with which we look up at the night sky.  
Through their voices and melodies, they share moments of calm, play, and dreams — small stories carried by sound and light.  
Their music drifts between lullabies, songs, and sound tales.

#### Philosophy

I believe that producing is not about control — it’s about translation.

Each song carries a certain light; my role is to help it shine through sound, space, and story.

Whether it’s a lullaby or an experimental track, I aim to create something that feels alive, honest, and timeless.
